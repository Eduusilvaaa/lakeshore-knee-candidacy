Is this call right for you?

A good fit if

  • Your knee has hurt for months, not days
  • Shots, pills or therapy helped for a while, then stopped
  • You want to know your options before agreeing to surgery
  • You're open to cash-pay care if you're a candidate

Probably not a fit if

  • You only want care covered by insurance
  • You just injured your knee. Please see urgent care or your doctor first
  • You live outside the Grand Rapids area and can't visit the office

Questions

Is the call really free?

Yes. The 15-minute candidacy call costs nothing and you're under no obligation.

Does insurance cover treatment?

Treatments are cash-pay, typically between $4,000 and $6,000 depending on your case. We go over the cost for your situation on the call.

Will you tell me I can skip surgery?

No one can promise that before an evaluation. The call tells you whether you're a candidate for a non-surgical option, and the in-office evaluation confirms it.

What happens after I submit the form?

We call you from a Grand Rapids (616) number at the time you picked. If we miss you, we text you to find a better time.

Where is the office?

Lakeshore Orthopedic & Regenerative is in Grand Rapids, Michigan. You'll get the address when your evaluation is booked.
